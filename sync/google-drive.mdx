## Purpose

Sync **configuration data** (models, prompts, custom models, and more) to Google Drive.

## First-time setup

1. Import / Export page → Google Drive sync
2. Authorize your Google account
3. Click sync

## Backup management

Open **Backup management** to view history files, restore, or delete specific backups.

## Revoke authorization

On the sync page you can **revoke Google authorization**. Local data is unaffected.
