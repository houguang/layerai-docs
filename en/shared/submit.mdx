The shared translation submission flow lets users publish completed whole-site translation packs to the community for others to download.

## Submission Entry Points

[Translation Management](/en/translation/site-list) → select a site → **Submit Shared** (100% progress).

See also [Submit Shared Translation](/en/translation/submit-shared).

## Review Workflow

```
Submit → Under review → Approved (published) / Rejected
```

Status and entry counts are visible in the **My Shares** view.

## Quality Guidelines

- Complete translations; avoid large numbers of empty entries
- Keep terminology aligned with official documentation
- Do not include illegal or infringing content

## After Publication

Other users can download the pack from the browse list. Submitters may request takedown through Layer operations if content violates policy.
