## Submission requirements

- [Logged in to a Layer account](/en/quickstart/login)
- Site translation progress reaches **100%**
- Entry quality meets community guidelines (no large numbers of empty translations)

## Steps

Translation management → select a site → **Submit to shared** → confirm.

After submission, the package enters review. Status can be checked under **Shared translations → My shared**:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Under review | Awaiting moderation |
| Approved | Published to the browse list |
| Rejected | Review the reason, fix issues, and resubmit |
