## Purpose

Syncs **configuration data** (models, prompts, custom models, etc.) to Google Drive.

<Note>
  By default this **does not include** translation site content—configuration only. For full translation backups, use [local JSON export](/en/sync/local-import-export) or [Layer Cloud Sync](/en/sync/layer-cloud).
</Note>

## First-Time Setup

1. Import/Export page → Google Drive Sync
2. Authorize the Google account
3. Run sync to create `LayerAI-sync-config.json` and timestamped backups

## Backup Management

**Backup Management** lists historical files and supports restore or deletion of specific backups.

## Revoke Authorization

Users can **revoke Google authorization** on the sync page without affecting local data.
