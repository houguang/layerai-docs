## Sign-in Methods

- Google OAuth
- Email registration / sign-in
- Other social sign-in options (as shown on the options page)

## Account Information

After signing in, the following is available under **Basic Settings**:

- Username / email
- Current plan (Free / Pro / Max)
- Token and shared download usage

## Customer Portal

Subscribed users can open **Customer Portal** from the [subscription page](https://local-layer.com/subscription) or the options page entry to manage:

- Renewal and cancellation
- Payment methods
- Invoices

## Sign Out

After signing out, local translation data is retained; cloud sync and shared translation features are unavailable.
