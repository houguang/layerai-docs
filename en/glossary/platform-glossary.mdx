## How to View

On the Glossary Management page, users can browse **platform glossary** categories and entries (read-only).

## Relationship to Local Glossaries

The platform glossary is maintained by Layer operations and suits industry-wide common terms. Local glossaries are for team-specific brand vocabulary.

During AI translation, active glossary entries from all available sources are applied together as constraints.

## Updates

The platform glossary cache updates when the extension syncs; no manual download is required.
