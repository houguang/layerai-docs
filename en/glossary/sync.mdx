## Requirements

Requires a **Pro or Max** plan and a signed-in Layer account.

## Operations

Options page → **Import/Export** → **Glossary Sync**:

- **Sync to cloud** — Upload the local glossary
- **Sync to local** — Pull from the cloud (overwrite or merge)

## Conflicts

Resolution follows the sync direction: upload uses local as source of truth; download uses the cloud. Users should [export a local backup](/en/sync/local-import-export) before important changes.
