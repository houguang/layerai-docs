A Layer account unlocks cloud sync, shared translations, platform models, and other collaboration features. Core capabilities—local collection, translation, and page replacement—do not require sign-in.

## Features Available After Sign-In

The following require a linked Layer account:

- **Layer cloud sync** (Pro / Max) — Sync translations, settings, and glossaries across devices
- **Shared translations** — Browse, download, and submit community translation packages
- **Platform models & quotas** — Use platform-provided AI models and Token quotas
- **Subscription management** — Pro / Max plan benefits

## Sign-In Methods

On the **Basic settings** page, users can choose:

- Google sign-in
- Email registration / sign-in
- Other social sign-in (as shown in the options page)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open basic settings">
    Extension popup → Manage → Basic settings
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose sign-in method">
    Click the corresponding button and complete OAuth or email verification in the browser
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm account status">
    The popup and basic settings page should show the username and plan badge (Free / Pro / Max)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Information Available After Sign-In

- **Token usage** — Free / Pro / Max model quotas
- **Shared download quota** — Used / remaining downloads this month
- **Customer Portal** — Manage subscription and payment methods (subscribed users)

See [Plans & Quotas](/en/account/plans) for details.

## Sign Out

After signing out in basic settings, **local translation data is retained**, but cloud sync and shared translation features are unavailable.
