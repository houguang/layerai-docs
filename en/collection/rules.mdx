## Automatic skip

The following are not collected again:

- Text that already has a matching translation locally
- Nodes in non-visible areas such as scripts or styles
- Inline structures covered by [protected HTML tags](/en/advanced/protected-tags)

## Placeholders

Variable placeholders on the page (e.g. `{{@id#1|source text}}`) keep their structure during AI translation; only the text after the pipe is translated. See [Appendix · Data format](/en/appendix/data-format).

## Sensitive information

When [sensitive masking](/en/advanced/sensitive-mask) is enabled, text sent to AI is masked according to rules first, reducing leak risk.

## Best practices

- Large sites in batches: core pages first, then sub-paths
- After collection, review the **Translation management** list and remove clearly incorrect entries
- Add brand terms to the [glossary](/en/glossary/overview) in advance
