Located under **Basic Settings** → **Shared Translation Settings**.

For full details, see [Shared Translation Discovery Modes](/en/shared/discover-modes).

## Quick Reference

- **Manual download** — confirmation dialog (recommended default)
- **Auto download** — suited for trusted sources and hands-off operation
- **Icon notification only** — no interruption; the dialog can be opened when needed

## Recommendations

- First-time shared translation users → choose manual download; switch to auto after verifying quality
- Internal enterprise sites → use auto download with caution
