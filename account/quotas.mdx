LayerAI allocates platform model tokens and shared translation download counts by plan. You can view current usage in Basic settings.

## Where to view

Basic settings → **Account** section.

## Token quotas

AI translation with **platform models** consumes tokens, allocated by plan:

- Free model pool
- Pro model pool
- Max model pool

**Custom models** use your own API quota and do not consume Layer tokens (unless traffic goes through a platform proxy).

## Shared download quotas

Monthly download limits for shared translation packs vary by plan. When the quota is exhausted, wait for the cycle to reset or upgrade your plan.

## When quotas are exhausted

- Tokens exhausted → switch to a [custom model](/models/custom-models) or upgrade your plan
- Download quota exhausted → wait for reset or use packs you already downloaded
