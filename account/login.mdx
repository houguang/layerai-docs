## Sign-in methods

- Google OAuth
- Email registration / sign-in
- Other social sign-in options (as shown on the extension management page)

## Account information

After signing in, check **Basic settings** for:

- Username / email
- Current plan (Free / Pro / Max)
- Token and shared-download usage

## Customer Portal

Subscribed users can open the **Customer Portal** from the [subscription page](https://local-layer.com/subscription) or the extension management entry to manage:

- Renewal and cancellation
- Payment methods
- Invoices

## Sign out

After signing out, local translation data remains. Cloud sync and shared features become unavailable.
