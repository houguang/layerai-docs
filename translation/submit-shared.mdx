## Requirements

- [Signed in to a Layer account](/journey/login)
- Site translation progress is **100%**
- Entry quality meets community guidelines (no large amounts of empty translations)

## Steps

Translation management → select a site → **Submit shared** → confirm.

After submission, the pack enters review. Check status under **Shared translations → My shares**:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| In review | Waiting for ops review |
| Approved | Published to the browse list |
| Rejected | Fix the issues and submit again |
