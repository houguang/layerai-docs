Located under **Basic settings** → **Shared translation settings**.

For full details, see [Shared translation discovery modes](/shared/discover-modes).

## Quick reference

- **Manual download** — confirm in a popup (recommended default)
- **Automatic download** — best when you trust the source and want zero friction
- **Icon badge only** — does not interrupt browsing; open the popup to download when needed

## Recommendations

- First time using shared features → choose manual download; switch to automatic after you trust the quality
- Internal enterprise sites → use automatic download cautiously
