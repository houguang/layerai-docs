A Layer account unlocks collaboration features such as cloud sync, shared translations, and platform models. Core features—local collection, translation, and page replace—do not require sign-in.

  <img src="/assets/images/ui-tour/9.png" alt="Account sign-in area in basic settings" />
</Frame>

## Features available after sign-in

These capabilities require an account:

- **Layer cloud sync** (Pro / Max) — Sync translations, settings, and glossaries across devices
- **Shared translations** — Download and share local translation packs
- **Platform models and quotas** — Use platform AI models and Token quotas
- **Subscription management** — Pro / Max plan benefits

## Sign-in methods

On the **Basic settings** page you can choose:

- Email registration / sign-in
- Other social sign-in options (as shown in the options page)

## Information available after sign-in

- **Token usage** — Free / Pro / Max model quotas
- **Shared download quota** — Used / remaining downloads this month
- **Membership** — Manage subscription and payment methods (for subscribed users)

## Sign out

After signing out in basic settings, **local translation data is retained**, but cloud sync and shared-translation features become unavailable.
