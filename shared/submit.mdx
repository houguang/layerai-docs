The shared translation submission flow lets you publish a completed full-site translation pack to the community for others to download.

## Submission entry

[Translation management](/translation/site-list) → select a site → **Submit share** (progress at 100%).

Or see [Submit a shared translation](/translation/submit-shared).

## Review process

```
Submit → Under review → Approved (published) / Rejected
```

In the **My shares** view, you can check status and entry counts.

## Quality guidelines

- Translate completely; avoid large numbers of empty lines
- Keep terminology aligned with official documentation
- Do not include illegal or infringing content

## After publishing

Other users can download the pack from the browse list. Submitters can also request takedown through the operations console (for example, if content violates policy).
