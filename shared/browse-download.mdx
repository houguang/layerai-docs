## In-page quick download

When you visit a site that has a matching community pack, the extension may prompt you per [discovery modes](/shared/discover-modes).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Discover">
    Open the site; a badge or prompt appears when a pack matches your discovery policy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Confirm download from the prompt, or open options → **Shared translations** and download there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use">
    With **auto-replace** on, the page applies the downloaded translations like local ones.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Download quotas

Free / Pro / Max plans have different **monthly download limits**. Check them under [Account quotas](/account/quotas). When the quota is exhausted, the UI shows the reset time.

## Likes

You can like a pack only after downloading it.
