## Requirements

- [Sign in to your Layer account](/journey/login)
- Remaining account token quota

## Select a model

Model configuration → **Default model** → choose a model marked Platform.

## View quotas

In Basic settings → **Account**, you can see:

- Free model tokens
- Pro model tokens
- Max model tokens

Quotas reset by plan and billing cycle. See [Quotas](/account/quotas).

## Troubleshooting

If your quota is exhausted or a model is unavailable, AI translation will fail. Switch to a [custom model](/models/custom-models) or upgrade your plan.
