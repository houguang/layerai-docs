Shared translations are LayerAI’s community collaboration feature: users submit reviewed whole-site packs for publication; others download them locally. Entry point: options page → **Shared translations**.

<Frame caption="Shared translations overview (~30 seconds)">
  <img src="/assets/images/modules/6.png" alt="Shared translations overview" />
</Frame>

| View | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Downloaded shares** | Packs you have downloaded |
| **My shares** | Submission history and review status |

Liking a pack requires downloading it first.

## Browse and download

<Frame caption="Prompt when a downloadable shared pack is available">
  <img src="/assets/images/ui-tour/2_1_cn.png" alt="Shared translation discovery prompt overlay" />

  <Step title="Discover">
    When visiting a site, matching packs are prompted per [discovery policy](/shared/discover-modes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download">
    Download from the popup or Shared translations page into the local library.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use">

  </Step>

## Discovery policy

In basic settings, choose how matching shared packs are handled when you visit a site: manual download, auto download, or badge-only reminder.

<Card title="Discovery policy" icon="radar" href="/shared/discover-modes">
  Comparison of the three discovery modes and where to configure them.
</Card>

## How it connects to other modules

A shared pack is essentially a published form of whole-site [Translation management](/modules/translation) data. After download, back it up via [Data sync](/modules/sync); before submit, unify terms with the [glossary](/modules/glossary).
