## Permissions

Requires a **Pro or Max** plan and a signed-in account.

## Actions

Extension management page → **Import / Export** → **Glossary sync**:

- **Sync to cloud** — upload the local glossary
- **Sync to local** — pull from the cloud to overwrite or merge

## Conflicts

The sync direction decides the winner: upload prefers local; download prefers cloud. Before important changes, [export a local backup](/sync/local-import-export).

## Troubleshooting

See [Sync error troubleshooting](/troubleshooting/sync-errors).
