## How to view

On the Glossary page, browse **Platform glossary** categories and entries (read-only).

## Relationship to the local glossary

Platform glossaries are maintained by operations and suit industry-common terms. Local glossaries are for private brand terms used by your team.

During AI translation, available active glossary entries are combined to constrain output.

## Updates

Platform glossaries refresh their cache when the extension syncs. No manual download is required.
