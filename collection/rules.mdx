## Automatic skip

These cases are not collected again:

- The text already has a matching translation locally
- The node is in non-visible areas such as scripts or styles
- Inline structures covered by the [protected HTML tags](/advanced/protected-tags) policy

## Placeholders

Variable placeholders on the page (for example `{{@id#1|source text}}`) keep their structure during AI translation; only the text after the pipe is translated. See [Appendix · Data format](/appendix/data-format).

## Sensitive information

If [sensitive masking](/advanced/sensitive-mask) is enabled, text sent to the AI is masked by rule first to reduce leakage risk.

## Best practices

- For large sites, work in batches: core pages first, then subpaths
- After collection, review the **Translation management** list and remove obvious false positives
- Add brand terms to the [glossary](/modules/glossary) ahead of time
